# Choosing Under Uncertainty

**By KK · Complete Chinese first draft · draft v0.1 · Content accepted by author**

The manuscript and rights notice retain their original first-draft wording. The current content-review status is stated here and in the metadata.

*Choosing Under Uncertainty* by KK is a complete Chinese first draft about ordinary decisions under incomplete information. Twelve original, story-led chapters cover framing a decision, setting constraints, developing options, assessing evidence, expressing uncertainty, considering intuition and emotion, using advice, comparing costs, running bounded trials, committing, changing course, and reviewing outcomes.

The manuscript includes a decision record, a bilingual terminology list, and eight source cards. It does not offer personalized investment advice or major medical decision instruction. The author has accepted the content of this AI-assisted draft; it is not a professionally finalized edition. Scenarios are fictional; research claims have traceable references and stated limits. A complete AI-assisted [English translation preview](translations/en/book.md) is now available; native-English review is pending.
